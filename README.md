# BOIRO Security

This repository is the central entry point for reporting security vulnerabilities affecting BOIRO products and services.

## Report a vulnerability

Please use [GitHub's private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/Boiro-io/security/security/advisories) to submit a report.

**Do not disclose security vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues, discussions, pull requests, or other public channels.**

A useful report should include:

- the affected product, service, domain, or endpoint;
- a clear description of the vulnerability and its potential impact;
- reproducible steps or a minimal proof of concept;
- any prerequisites, test accounts, or configuration involved;
- relevant logs, screenshots, or request and response data with secrets and personal data removed;
- a preferred way for us to contact you.

## Scope

Reports may cover BOIRO-operated products, applications, APIs, and web services. Third-party services and software are only in scope when the issue is caused by BOIRO's configuration or integration.

If you are unsure whether an asset is in scope, submit a private report before performing further testing.

## Testing guidelines

When investigating a potential vulnerability:

- use accounts and data you own or have explicit permission to use;
- avoid accessing, changing, downloading, or deleting other users' data;
- do not perform denial-of-service, social-engineering, phishing, spam, or destructive testing;
- minimize collection and retention of personal or confidential information;
- stop testing and report immediately if you encounter sensitive data or gain unintended access;
- do not publicly disclose the vulnerability before BOIRO has had a reasonable opportunity to investigate and remediate it.

## What happens after a report

BOIRO will review the report, assess its impact and reproducibility, and coordinate next steps with the reporter. We may request additional information during the investigation and will communicate when remediation or coordinated disclosure is appropriate.

Submitting a report does not guarantee a monetary reward. Any recognition or reward is determined by BOIRO on a case-by-case basis.

Thank you for helping keep BOIRO and its users secure.
